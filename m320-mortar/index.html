How to use it

  1. Open the map (enable navigation aid) and read your grid and the target grid. 4-digit per axis is plenty.
  2. Enter both points above. You get distance, bearing and two possible elevations.
  3. Turn to the bearing with your compass (K). The map line from you to your marker shows the same bearing.
  4. Look through the Cobra rangefinder. It shows your vertical angle; tilt up until it reads the elevation.
  5. Fire with the launcher held at that same angle. Spot the impact with RGE or a drone and adjust.

The high angle (above 45°) arcs like a real mortar and clears cover. The low angle is quicker in the air but needs a clear line.
